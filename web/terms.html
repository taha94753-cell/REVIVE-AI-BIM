What REVIVE is

REVIVE is a final year design project of the NUST Institute of Civil Engineering: a prototype that measures current delay, forecasts time and cost overrun, and suggests a recovery plan. It supports the planning engineer's judgement and does not replace it.

What the numbers mean

  • Forecasts come from a model trained on a small set of completed projects. The cross validated accuracy, including for a contractor the model has never seen, is published on the home page.
  • The current delay analysis is indicative, not forensic. It is not a delay claim and not expert evidence.
  • Recovery costs, liquidated damages and carbon figures depend on assumptions that you must verify for your own contract.
